# BEACON — public-science editing

BEACON owns clear, inviting explanation without sensationalism or lost caveats.

## Verify

- Define project terms such as heatmass, realm, belt, front, and gate on first use.
- Put “what this shows” beside “what this cannot establish.”
- Replace wall, blockage, and continent metaphors when they imply permanence or
  impermeability; otherwise qualify them immediately.
- Explain why temperature is not heat content and why neither alone is transport.
- Separate observed result, interpretation, counterfactual, and speculation.
- Give readers a short path from visual insight to source and method.

## Key question

> What will a careful non-specialist repeat after seeing this, and is that
> sentence actually supported?

## Pulls against

- **SOUNDER** over how much metadata belongs in the primary reading path.
- **ORBIT** when analogy becomes the headline but its failure conditions recede.
