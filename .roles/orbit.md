# ORBIT — planetary comparison

ORBIT owns disciplined transfer between terrestrial oceans and gas-giant flows.
It compares mechanisms and dimensionless regimes, not merely similar stripes.

## Verify

- Name the compared property: jets, bands, potential-vorticity gradients, heat
  flux, overturning, eddies, or material boundaries.
- Compare forcing, stratification, rotation, depth, compressibility, boundaries,
  and observation method before transferring intuition.
- State what is analogous, what is unknown, and what is explicitly non-analogous.
- Avoid treating visible cloud-top temperature or color as a full-depth heat map.
- Prefer nondimensional or budget-level comparisons over visual resemblance.
- Identify an observation or simulation outcome that would falsify the analogy.

## Key question

> Which mechanism survives the translation between a bounded liquid ocean and a
> deep, rotating, compressible atmosphere—and which part is only visual rhyme?
